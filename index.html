Rural Rise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Rural Rise is developed by Kibee03. For privacy requests or game support, contact theruralrisesupport@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

Rural Rise stores progress, achievements, settings and earned cosmetics in your device's app or browser storage. The current game does not require a Rural Rise account or upload your save to a game server. Android may include app data in device backups according to your device settings.

Optional local play record

You may enable a small diagnostic record in Festival Gardens. It records selected game actions and connection results on your device. This record is off by default. You can export it yourself, clear it, or disable it and clear its entries in the game. The game does not automatically upload this record.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android build includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. The current development build requests test advertisements. Google services may process technical device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This build removes advertising-ID and Android ad-services permissions. We do not request access to your precise GPS location, contacts, microphone or camera for advertising.

Where Google requires privacy choices, it manages them before eligible ad requests. You can reopen a required privacy-options form from the Village Shop's Advertising privacy choices button. Such a form may not be required when under-age-of-consent treatment applies. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's handling of its service data.

Children and families

The intended audience includes children and older players. On Android, a neutral age screen asks for a date of birth before the game starts. The date is processed on your device and is not saved or sent to Rural Rise. Only a broad under-13 or older age group and the date of the check are stored on your device. The game asks again after 30 days; the earlier local record is replaced when you answer. You can clear this local record using Android's Clear storage setting. Every player receives child-directed ad treatment and UMP's under-age-of-consent setting; we do not ask children to consent to personalized advertising. Ad requests are configured for non-personalized, general-audience advertising. The game currently uses no third-party mediation networks. These settings are development safeguards; they do not mean that the game has completed Google's Families review.

Parents or guardians can contact us at the support address above about a child's data. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support rather than send personal information themselves.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are not enabled in the current test build. No Ads Forever and Village VIP are previews. We will update this policy to describe purchase verification and its data handling before enabling paid products.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use it to respond and troubleshoot your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents. We retain support messages only for as long as needed for the request and any applicable obligations; you may ask us to delete them.

You can clear game progress through the game's reset controls. To remove all app-local data, use Android's Clear storage setting or your browser's site-data controls. Google manages data collected by its services under its own policy and privacy controls.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
